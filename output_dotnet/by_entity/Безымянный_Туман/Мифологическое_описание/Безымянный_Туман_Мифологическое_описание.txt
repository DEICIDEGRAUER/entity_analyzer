--- Delta Green (setting).txt ---

[1] The Unspeakable L69
Premise Delta Green is a top-secret, black-ops organisation dedicated to combatting the threats presented by various Cthulhu Mythos creatures and cults from the shadows. Originally taking place in a 1990s setting, it has since been updated to reflect contemporary times, equipment and issues, and expanded to cover other time periods, such as the Vietnam War. Development Created by John Scott Tynes Dennis Detwiller and Adam Scott Glancy , the very first appearance of Delta Green was in a roleplaying game scenario in the Cthulhu Mythos gaming fanzine The Unspeakable Oath , by Pagan Publishing . The scenario was titled "Convergence" and was released in 1992. Pagan followed this up in 1997 with a complete sourcebook for the setting, which itself spawned a series of expansions. In 2016, Arc Dream Publishing released a stand-alone roleplaying game based on Delta Green, which was also accompanied by a wealth of satellite books.
