НАЗВАНИЕ: The Horror in the Gallery ИСТОЧНИК: ДАТА: 2026-06-15 03:31:30 СИМВОЛОВ: 6267 This article is for the short story "Zoth-Ommog", aka "The Horror in the Gallery"; for the Great Old One see Zoth-Ommog "The Horror in the Gallery" , originally published under the title "Zoth-Ommog" in the 1976 anthology The Disciples of Cthulhu , is a Cthulhu Mythos short story by Lin Carter , and part of his Xothic Legend Cycle Great Old Ones and the Elder Gods , based on and including references to the works of several Mythos authors. Synopsis The story is presented as a police testimony given by Arthur Wilcox Hodgkins in 1929, after a night watchman is found murdered in the South Gallery of the Sanbourne Institute of Pacific Antiquities in Santiago, California. Hodgkins maintains that there were actually two bodies, the second being the body of the night watchman's murderer.
