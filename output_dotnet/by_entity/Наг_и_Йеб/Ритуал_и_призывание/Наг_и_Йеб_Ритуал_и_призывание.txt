It was published in the thematic anthology Historical Lovecraft by Innsmouth Free Press in April 2011. Synopsis In the aftermath of the unexplained assassination of a senior Soviet politician, man named Yezhov is tasked with uncovering the motive for this attack on the State. He soon uncovers a plot by mystical forces to interfere in the affairs of humankind and, after discovering the entity controlling the human assassin - a monstrous Lloigor - embarks on a course of action which he believes will result in the purging of all supernatural elements from his country. The story is interspersed with a series of missives between R'lyeh and the Leningrad Operational Center. These reveal that the initial assassination attempt was the result of poorly-planned meddling in human affairs; the final one explains how the manipulation of Yezhov and the sacrifice of a Lloigor agent had resulted in a violent program which would keep the secrets of the supernatural puppet masters safe for centuries.
