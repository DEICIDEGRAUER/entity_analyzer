François Honoré-Balfour , Cultes des Goules Cultes des Goules , or Cults of Ghouls , is a fictional book of black magic. In the Cthulhu Mythos , it is said to have been written by François Honoré-Balfour ( Comte d'Erlette ) in 1702. It was published in France and later denounced by the Church. Only a handful of copies are in existence. One of the known copies was kept for 91 years in an arcane library of the Church of Starry Wisdom in Providence, Rhode Island . After Robert Blake 's mysterious death in 1935, Doctor Dexter removed the grimoire and added it to his library.
